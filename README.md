# Max Personal Assistant Legal Pages

Static legal pages for Max Personal Assistant, a private account-owner-only productivity assistant used for low-volume reminders, tasks, calendar notices, and direct-request responses.

Public URLs for Twilio A2P review:

- Privacy Policy: https://majeronid.github.io/max-personal-assistant/privacy.html
- Terms and Conditions: https://majeronid.github.io/max-personal-assistant/terms.html

These pages are intentionally non-promotional and state that Max is not used for marketing, lead generation, patient communication, PHI, or third-party outreach.
