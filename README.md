# Max Personal Assistant Legal Pages

Static legal pages for Max Personal Assistant, a private account-owner-only productivity assistant used for low-volume reminders, tasks, calendar notices, and direct-request responses.

Public URLs for Twilio A2P review:

- Privacy Policy: https://majeronid.github.io/max-personal-assistant/privacy.html
- Terms and Conditions: https://majeronid.github.io/max-personal-assistant/terms.html
- SMS Compliance / A2P Program Details: https://majeronid.github.io/max-personal-assistant/compliance.html

These pages are intentionally non-promotional and state that Max is not used for marketing, lead generation, patient communication, PHI, or third-party outreach.

## A2P positioning

- Program type: private account-owner-only productivity assistant.
- Audience: one opted-in account owner on an allowlisted phone number.
- Message type: low-volume reminders, calendar/task notices, health/fitness coaching reminders requested by the account owner, system delivery alerts, and direct responses.
- Consent: account owner texts the assistant number directly or explicitly authorizes reminders during private setup.
- Opt-out: STOP/CANCEL/END/QUIT/UNSUBSCRIBE/STOPALL.
- Help: HELP/INFO.
- Consent sharing: SMS consent is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
