Max Personal Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Max Personal Assistant is a private, account-owner-only productivity assistant. It is used to send low-volume reminders, task updates, calendar notices, and direct-request responses to the account owner.

Information We Collect

Max Personal Assistant may process the account owner’s phone number, message content, reminder requests, calendar/task context, and basic message delivery metadata needed to send and receive SMS or voice notifications.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide requested reminders, task updates, personal productivity notifications, and direct responses to the account owner. Messages are one-to-one, non-promotional, and not used for marketing, lead generation, patient communication, or third-party outreach.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by communications providers, such as Twilio, solely to deliver SMS or voice messages. Information may also be used as required to maintain security, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable law.

SMS Consent

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. The service is private and intended only for the account owner, who opts in inside the account settings of the Max Personal Assistant application by entering their phone number and confirming it with a one-time verification code. The full opt-in flow is documented on the SMS Opt-In Process page.

Opt Out

The account owner may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or STOPALL. Reply START to resume messages where supported. Reply HELP or INFO for assistance.

A2P / SMS Program Details

Program details, sample messages, consent language, and opt-out instructions are available on the SMS Compliance / A2P Program Details page.

Data Retention

Message and task data may be retained only as long as needed to provide the assistant service, maintain logs, troubleshoot delivery, or comply with legal obligations.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect assistant data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This service is not directed to children and is intended only for the account owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the account owner or administrator of Max Personal Assistant.
