Max Personal Assistant SMS Compliance / A2P Program Details

Effective date: June 6, 2026

Max Personal Assistant is a private, account-owner-only personal productivity assistant. This A2P 10DLC compliance summary documents consent, opt-out, help, sample-message, and use-case language for Twilio review.

It sends low-volume, one-to-one SMS reminders and direct responses only to the account owner who explicitly opts in.

Program Description

Messages may include private reminders, calendar notices, task updates, health/fitness coaching reminders requested by the account owner, delivery/status alerts for the assistant system, and direct answers to inbound requests from the account owner.

Audience

The audience is limited to the account owner. The service is not a public signup list, marketing program, lead generation program, patient communication workflow, or bulk messaging service.

Opt-In Method

The account owner opts in by texting the assistant number directly, saving the number as the private assistant contact, or explicitly authorizing the assistant during private setup. The assistant is configured with an allowlist so messages are limited to the account owner.

Opt-Out and Help

Reply STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or STOPALL to opt out. Reply START to resume messages where supported. Reply HELP or INFO for help.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on reminders, calendar activity, tasks, and account-owner requests. Typical volume is low and personal, often a few reminders or status messages per day.

Sample Messages

Max: Morning precision day. Protein first, sync Garmin, and take an 8-minute glucose cleanup walk after breakfast. Reply DONE when complete.

Max: Reminder: 9:15 PM wind-down. Parasympathetic mode, screens down, tomorrow-David wins.

Max: Hermes cron health found a delivery issue and switched your reminder fallback to Telegram. No action needed.

Data and Consent Sharing

SMS consent is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Service providers such as Twilio may process message metadata only as needed to deliver the service.

Disallowed Use

Max Personal Assistant is not used for emergency services, public marketing, political messaging, lead generation, patient outreach, or third-party bulk messaging.
