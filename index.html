Max Personal Assistant

SMS opt-in process and program disclosures. Last updated: June 10, 2026

What Max Is

Max Personal Assistant is a private, account-owner-only personal productivity assistant. It sends low-volume, one-to-one SMS reminders, calendar notices, task updates, and direct responses only to the account owner who explicitly opts in. It is not a public signup list, marketing program, or bulk messaging service.

How Opt-In Works

SMS opt-in occurs inside the account settings of the Max Personal Assistant application and requires verification of the phone number:

  1. The account owner opens Account Settings in the Max Personal Assistant application.
  2. The account owner enters their mobile phone number and requests SMS notifications.
  3. A one-time verification code is sent to that phone number by SMS.
  4. The account owner enters the verification code in account settings to confirm ownership of the number and complete opt-in.

At the point of opt-in, the account owner is shown the following consent disclosure:

By verifying your number you agree to receive personal reminder and assistant messages from Max Personal Assistant at the phone number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

Only verified, opted-in numbers belonging to the account owner receive messages. SMS consent is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Program Disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on reminders, calendar activity, and account-owner requests; typical volume is low and personal.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply HELP for help at any time.
  • Reply STOP to cancel and unsubscribe at any time.
